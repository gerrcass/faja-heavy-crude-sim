# A single interactive field guide

The audience is a systems engineer who wants a working mental model of the Orinoco production chain, not a policy briefing or a reservoir-engineering course. We will publish one long-form interactive field guide with a single Hero cutaway and one system simulator, rather than a multi-page course or a collection of unrelated figures. A single page keeps the causal chain — geology to lift to blend to upgrade — in one view, and gives the Anatomy figure a clear job: it is the system map the prose keeps pointing back to.
